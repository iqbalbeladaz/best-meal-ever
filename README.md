## The entry

> The best meal I ever had was rendang at my grandmother's house in Jakarta, eaten cross-legged on the floor as rain hammered the tin roof and nobody spoke, the beef too good to interrupt.

One sentence, 186 characters (gate: 1–200).

## Why this sentence should win

- **Concrete in every clause:** a named dish (rendang), a place
  (grandmother's house in Jakarta), a posture (cross-legged on the floor),
  a sound (rain hammering the tin roof) — not general praise.
- **True to one person's memory:** the details are too specific to be a
  stock line anyone could have written; it could only be this person's
  grandmother, this house, this rain.
- **Reads aloud well:** one clean sentence, fresh wording, no cliche — the
  rhythm carries from the dish to the rain to the silence.
- **The ending stays:** a whole room silenced by good beef is the image
  that lingers after a single reading.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. It is fully
deterministic: the same bytes on every run, no input, no randomness, no
network.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
